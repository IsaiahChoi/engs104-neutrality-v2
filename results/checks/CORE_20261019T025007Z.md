## Finding A: K9 FAIL, the perspective-relaxation estimator on valid toys in general

**What passes.** The value function and the big-M and fixed-integer estimators pass on every toy. The perspective-relaxation dual (Clarabel, per revised DESIGN §6) passes on the two toys in the test and config (T1 ≤ 5.0e-7, T2 ≤ 3.4e-7).

**What fails.** I read K9 as a claim about the estimator on any valid toy (0 < ℓ < w° < u), as in round 1 and as the caller asked. The claim does not hold at 1e-6 relative.
- Sample: 200 random valid toys.
  - a ∈ [1e-4, 1], λ ∈ [0.5, 50], w° ∈ [3e-4, 0.3], log-uniform.
  - Each toy is built exactly as run_core `_toy_pmi` / tests `toy_pmi` build it.
  - Grids have 41 points.
- Misses: 58 of 7,700 off-kink points, on 49 of 200 toys. The worst is 4.3e-5 relative.
  - With kinks on the grid: 15 points, max 5.9e-6.
  - Generic toys: 43 points, max 4.3e-5.
- Where the misses fall:
  - Every miss is in the region ℓ < b < w°, where the relaxation optimum has z = 1. Below ℓ and above w° the error is ≤ 4.2e-10.
  - Every miss is a Clarabel AlmostSolved. Where Clarabel reports Solved, the maximum is 7.0e-7.
- How large the misses are:
  - 17 misses have |μ| ≥ 0.1·a.
  - 3 exceed 1e-6·a in absolute terms. The worst is toy 34 at b = 8.0e-4: closed form 3.4245e-3, module 3.4244e-3, error 2.5e-5 relative.
- Realistic magnitudes are affected too. On toys with u ≤ 6%, ℓ ≥ 0.1% and f(w°) ∈ [1e-6, 1e-2], 14 of 2,107 points miss (max 1.5e-5).

**The closed form is right, and the accuracy is attainable.**
- In this region the big-M dual equals the same closed form a − 2λdb to ≤ 1e-13.
- My own minimal one-asset Clarabel relaxation, with weights scaled by u, gets 2e-8 to 2e-7 at the three worst points I checked (k9_spot_out.json).
- The miss therefore belongs to the module's relaxation as built for the toy: AlmostSolved is accepted, and the cardinality row Σζ ≤ K = 1 duplicates ζ ≤ 1, a degenerate active set.

**The error is deterministic but erratic.**
- With bit-identical inputs it reproduces exactly (k9_repro_out.json).
- Perturbing the inputs in the last bits (a CSV round trip) moves it unpredictably: from 2.5e-5 to 2.7e-7 on one point, and from 3.9e-5 to 6.6e-5 on another.
- Changing s_obj also moves it: on these points, s_obj from 1 to 1e8 gives errors between 9e-9 and 5.9e-6 (k9_spot_module_out.json).
- So the 1e-6 accuracy on T1 and T2 is not a property of the method.

**What I did not do.** Whether to change the method or the tolerance is Isaiah's decision. I did not try fixes.

**Implication beyond CORE.** K29b in S3 asks for production perspective duals to agree to 1e-6 at s_obj and 10·s_obj, and AlmostSolved is common: 29 of 74 K9 points in the run, and both gated K8 relaxations at s_obj = 1e4 (both were Solved at s = 1).
