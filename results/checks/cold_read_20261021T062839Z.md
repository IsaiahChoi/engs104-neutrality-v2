2. **TOP 5 #2. Family-level duals and elasticities are undefined.**

   - But the Q1 headline numbers are per family: p4 "liquidity cap binds at all 12 dates with median elasticity 0.16", "sector bounds at 12 (median 0.014)".

## 6. Three questions I would ask in Q&A

1. The default-scaling perspective's root gap (0.015) is about 30× the measured cone-violation effect (4.6e-4) and worse than its own plain relaxation. What direct evidence says absolute cone tolerance is the cause, rather than Gurobi's MIQCP handling changing with scaling? Did you vary the feasibility tolerance or the MIQCP method directly?
2. P-MI fixes λ* from P-RB, so its optimum need not sit at 3%/yr. How far below the budget does it fall? Do the Q2 sensitivities, which are of a penalized objective, still mean "alpha per unit of bound at equal risk"?
3. Support changes occur in 16–17 of 40 I2 intervals, P3 covers 24 of 34 cases, and no estimator lands in [L, H] in I2. Which estimator should a pod actually use, and is a ±10% secant the right target? Also, how do you aggregate per-row multipliers into one family elasticity, such as the 0.16 for θ?
