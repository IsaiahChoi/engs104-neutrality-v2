The CMA bound stops binding inside the 1% step, and smaller steps converge to μ.
2. **The K18 reading.** CHECKS K18 says "A*(unconstrained) − A*(base) … per the identity attribution.py claims". attribution.py claims α'w* = α'w_α + Σ_c α'w_c, so I took the right-hand side as α'w_α − α'w*. Two other readings do not hold, and both gaps are already documented. (a) The fixed-risk loss A_unc − A*: the summed losses exceed it by at least 6.2e-4 at every binding point, which is the gap DESIGN §10 states for Fig. 3. (b) Net alpha, α'w_α − A*: it differs by up to 9.7e-4, the borrow and trading costs. If Isaiah intends either of these, K18 would FAIL under that reading.
3. **The `inactive` term is large at some points (non-gating).** At binding points it carries up to 0.186 of loss, or 11.8% of the total. At the slack α∥ points it carries up to 9.7e3, out of totals of 1.2e5–1.8e5, because the λ = 1e-6 attribution is degenerate. The term is stated and the identity holds, but it is a sizeable share for the Fig. 3 discussion.
4. **K21 scope.** The K15 stencil solves do not store a risk flag of their own. My recomputation of all of them (230 coarse, plus 128 fine) found every one risk-binding.
5. **Slack points.** Stored A* at slack points comes from the QP at λ_min, which can sit up to λ_min·σ_m² ≈ 7.5e-11 below the true P-RB value. Measured gaps against Clarabel are ≤ 2.5e-11 relative, far inside every tolerance.
