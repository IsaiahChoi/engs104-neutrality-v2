## 7. Three Q&A questions

1. With λ* held fixed, P-MI's volatility ranges from 2.22% to 3.31%, so every Q2 slope is a slope of the penalized objective, not of alpha at equal risk. Would the estimator ranking (perspective vs fixed-integer vs big-M) survive if you re-rooted λ at each perturbed point? Given n = 23 cases on 3 dates (and n = 11 at a single date for the δ-dependence claim), how general is "the best estimator depends on the step"?
2. The rescaled perspective changes both the row scaling (÷ max u_i) and the per-name cone scaling. Which one fixes the root gap and the incumbent inflation? Did you run a cone-only rescaling? Relatedly, what does Gurobi's presolve actually do to the big-M model to cut its root gap from 0.057 to 6×10⁻⁴: bound tightening on M = u_i?
3. The risk budget is slack in 4 α∥ base solves. How does Brent root-finding on log λ handle a slack risk row, and what λ* and duals are reported there? Also, why ε_p ≥ 10⁻⁴ as the binding threshold, and do the Q1 binding counts change at 10⁻³?
