Sections II–III are unusually complete. The model, data, the two formulations and the A1–A9 derivations can be followed and checked by hand; I verified A2–A4, A7, A9 and the 8η stencil bound. IV-A and IV-B are mostly traceable. Self-containment breaks in four places:

## 7. Three Q&A questions

1. **What is the right target for the comparison?** Your "true marginal cost" is a ±10% secant across a value function you show is discontinuous and nonconcave (P3, 18 of 23 cases). The perspective slope is a ±0.1% local derivative of a relaxation. How much of each estimator's "error" is just derivative-vs-secant mismatch? Is there a reason to prefer the secant as the target over, say, the expected slope over a δ distribution?
2. **Does fixing λ* change the Q2 conclusions?** λ* is fixed per (date, N), and P-MI's volatility drifts from 2.22 to 3.31%/yr, so Q2 measures slopes of a penalized objective, not equal-risk costs. Would the estimator ranking survive if λ were re-root-found per P-MI solve, so that integer slopes were at equal risk like Q1's?
3. **Did Gurobi actually choose outer approximation?** The inference rests on MIQCPMethod 1 reproducing the default bound at 29 of 36. What did the default do at the other 7? Can the Gurobi log confirm the choice directly? And is the "two mechanisms" finding a property of the formulation or of one solver version's defaults?
