## 1. Verdict: MOSTLY self-contained

Almost every symbol in Sections II–IV is defined before or near its first use. I could reproduce A1–A7, the interval (3) in A8, both numbers in A9, and the finite-difference noise bounds by hand, and found no errors. Four things stop it being fully self-contained:
- **Internal labels are left in the text.** "K29b", "K29c", "CHECKS" and "S2d" appear in Sec. III-E and Sec. IV-C, and a reader cannot recover the tolerances or checks they stand for.
- **Solve counts and samples are hard to trace.** Table II's totals and several n values can only be rebuilt by guessing.
- **Some finance terms are named but never explained.** These are "information coefficient", "books", "adjusted prices", "backtest" and "loading units".
- **Some captions or set-ups disagree with the text.** Table III's caption contradicts its baseline row, and the I1 Mom bound is 0.025 against 0.10 in Table I with no reason given.

## 4. Derivations

**Checked and correct:**
- **A1.** IVT root existence; KKT at λ*; Slater via w = 0 with affine remaining rows; λmin·σm² = 10⁻⁶ × (0.03²/12) = 7.5 × 10⁻¹¹; the near-optimality bound f(w) ≤ f(w_min) + λmin·σm².
- **A2–A3.** w* = Σ⁻¹g/(2λ); ν; λ = √(g⊤Σ⁻¹g)/(2σm); A*eq = σm√(g⊤Σ⁻¹g); g⊤Σ⁻¹α = g⊤Σ⁻¹g; Cauchy–Schwarz; equality with TC.
- **A4.** ΣD⁻¹α⊥ = α⊥; vanishing cross term; √(1−φ).
- **A6.** Checks.
- **A7.** Both relaxation slopes check, including q = ℓw at z = w/ℓ, and a − 2λdb < a − λdℓ for b > ℓ/2.
- **A8, interval (3).** L ≤ secant ≤ H checks, as does the nonconcavity test V(k−1) + V(k+1) > 2Ūeff(k) on the equal-spaced grid.
- **A9.** 10⁻⁶/ℓ² = 0.16; 10⁻⁶(0.06/0.0025)² = 5.76 × 10⁻⁴.
- **Finite differences.** Central-difference noise ≤ ε/δr = η. The coefficients of s+ − s− are (−6, 4, 4, −1, −1), absolute sum 16, giving a bound of 8η.

**Steps I could only take on trust:**
- **A1:** "volatility continuous, nonincreasing in λ". This is asserted, not shown. One line would cover it: comparing the optimality conditions at λ1 < λ2 gives monotonicity, and uniqueness gives continuity.
- **A2–A3:** the labels A2 and A3 are never told apart in the combined paragraph. "Full row rank" is needed for (AΣ⁻¹A⊤)⁻¹; that is fine, but the closed form used for mandate (ii) (A = [B 1]⊤) is not written out. IV-A says closed form and numerics agree at n = 48, which includes (ii).
- **A5:** "also across nondegenerate active-set changes, where µk has a kink". Plausible, but no argument is given. Concavity in θ and u needs the composition of a nondecreasing concave function with the concave ui = min(u, θADVi/AUM). The 312-sweep concavity check depends on this, and A5 does not say it.
- **A8:** why +10⁻⁶|V| is the right margin when MIPGap is 10⁻⁴ (N1) is not justified. "A margin, not a proof" is honest, but it leaves "certified" resting on that choice.
- **A9:** "so their gap comes from the cone effect". This is inferred from the solves ending OPTIMAL, not derived.
- **IV-C:** the root-bound mechanism is explicitly "consistent with" outer approximation, with the logs off. It is fine as stated, but the Abstract and Conclusion outlines should keep the word "inferred".
- **Finite differences:** the noise premise ignores error from the other rows' residuals (up to 10⁻⁸). The text concedes the premise is unproven. Truncation error of the stencils is not discussed; it is probably negligible at δr = 10⁻³b. The kink flag is defined but never used in Results, so I cannot see its role.

**Nothing looked mathematically wrong.**
