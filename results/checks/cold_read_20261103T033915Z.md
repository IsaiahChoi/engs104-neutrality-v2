6. THREE QUESTIONS I WOULD ASK IN Q&A
=====================================================================
1. P-MI fixes λ* from the convex problem, and its volatility then drifts from 2.22% to 3.31%. So Q2's "true marginal cost" is the slope of a risk-penalized objective, not the fixed-budget cost that Q1 and the Intro argue for. Does any Q2 conclusion carry over to the platform's actual question, and why not root-find λ for P-MI too?
2. At δ = 10%, 18 of 23 cases change support within the step, only 4 of 23 estimates fall in [L, H], and the step-size reversal rests on one date (2017-12, n = 11). What should a platform risk manager actually use, which estimator at which δ, and how do you know the reversal is not specific to 2017-12?
3. Forced outer approximation reproduces the root bound at 29 of 36 default-scaling instances, but also at 30 of 36 rescaled ones, where the root gap is small. What about scaling makes outer approximation "stop early" only in the default arm, and with logs off, what evidence separates that from other root-cut or presolve effects?
