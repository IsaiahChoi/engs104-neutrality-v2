## 1. Verdict: MOSTLY self-contained

Sections II–IV define nearly every symbol before the results use it. I could reproduce A1–A9 and the finite-difference noise bound by hand, with the small gaps listed in §4. Most counts reconcile once you do the arithmetic yourself: C1 108, C2 2040, I1 288, I2 164, N1 147, N3 252, 312 sweeps, 240 relaxations. The report falls short of "self-contained" for four reasons:
- Internal labels whose meaning or tolerance is never given: K29b, K29c, S2d, CHECKS, "design pilot".
- Several statistics with no sample or units: validation agreements, N2(b) errors, Work values, "144 pairs".
- Ambiguities in the I1/I2 design: the Mom bound at 0.025, how the bounds were chosen, and f for K = 67/79.
- Some runs are announced and never reported: mandate (ii), the kink flag, and Clarabel/SCS in N2(b).

## 4. Derivations

- **A1:** The Lagrangian includes "− ν⊤(mandate)" but is then maximized "over the mandate". That mixes a full and a partial Lagrangian; state that only the risk row is dualized.
  - "Otherwise the budget is slack (λ* = 0)" is not exactly right. The volatility at λmin can be within budget while λ* lies in (0, 10⁻⁶). The 7.5×10⁻¹¹ bound still holds; I checked σm² × 10⁻⁶ = 7.5×10⁻¹¹.
  - Continuity of the volatility in λ, and its monotonicity, are asserted without a one-line reason.
- **A2–A3:** I reproduced w*, ν, λ and A*eq. "At most one by Cauchy–Schwarz" needs the missing step g⊤Σ⁻¹g = g⊤Σ⁻¹α, which follows from the Σ⁻¹-orthogonality of A⊤ν and g. With that step Cauchy–Schwarz works; without it, it reads like a Pythagoras/projection argument. The label "A2–A3" never says which part is A2 and which is A3.
- **A4:** Reproduced: B⊤D⁻¹α⊥ = 0, Σ⁻¹α⊥ = D⁻¹α⊥, the vanishing cross term, and √(1−φ). It also shows φ ∈ [0,1], which II-C uses without pointing here.
- **A5:** Fine. One might note that concavity in θ and u comes from ui = min(u, θADV/AUM) being concave and nondecreasing in them; the 312-sweep concavity check relies on this.
- **A6:** Uses z where it means ζ. At ζ = 0 the cone gives w = 0 and q ≥ 0, not q = 0 (q is minimized to 0).
- **A7:** Reproduced the jump and both slopes. The perspective slope a − λdℓ holds only for b < ℓ (and the big-M slope needs b ≤ u); please state the range. The ℓ/2 < b < ℓ comparison checks out.
- **A8:** Interval (3) checks out. The 10⁻⁶|V| margin is not motivated, and the "per point" violation language is unclear (see §3 item 11).
- **A9:** Checked 10⁻⁶/ℓ² = 0.16 and 10⁻⁶(0.06/0.0025)² = 5.76×10⁻⁴ ≈ 5.8×10⁻⁴. It is unclear how the extra division by max_i u_i interacts with the per-name cone scaling.
- **III-D noise:** Reproduced η = ε/δr, the coefficients of s+ − s− (6+4+4+1+1 = 16), and the 8η bound. Nothing wrong found.
- **IV-B:** "affine in b, so the value is quadratic": correct; the central secant of a quadratic equals the derivative at the midpoint.
